Our Story

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not

Colaberry was founded in 2012 on a simple belief: with the right guidance, anyone can build a transformative career in technology. We have spent over a decade closing the gap between ambition and opportunity, and today we are helping people become AI Systems Architects.

01Our mission

Built on inclusivity and empathy

We welcome learners from every economic, racial, and occupational background. Despite our differences, our community shares the strength of tenacity and resourcefulness.

Through project-based learning, mentorship, and a relentless focus on outcomes, we have helped thousands of people switch into the careers they deserve. No degree, no prior IT experience required.

02The next chapter

From data careers to AI systems

The work has changed. The mission has not. AI is rewriting every role, and the people who learn to build with it will have the advantage.

So we went to the source. A 10-person cohort of our team completed Anthropic Academy's four-course learning path (agent skills, the Claude API, Model Context Protocol, and Claude Code), earning Colaberry membership in the Claude Partner Network. The AI Systems Architect Accelerator is how we pass that on: learn with Claude, build real projects, deploy in the real world.
